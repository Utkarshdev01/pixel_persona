The load & release lab

Choose a pressure to change the illustration and its note. Switch Release to lift the weight. This is a visual pause—not a claim that one breath solves everything.

Marks & rank

Name one thing about yourself that a score cannot measure.

A result is information. It is not your worth.

JEE / NEET

Pick one question or concept to review—not the whole syllabus.

A mock test is practice data, not a prediction.

Relatives & comparison

Think of one sentence you can use to protect your privacy.

“I’m working on my own plan.” is enough.

Coaching & isolation

Choose one person to contact today: a friend, family member, teacher or warden.

You deserve company and support.

Placement anxiety

Ask your placement office for one concrete next step.

One application does not decide your future.

Fees & loans

Write down the next due date, then ask your college about available options.

You do not have to solve the finances alone.

Sleep & phone

Try charging your phone a little farther from the bed tonight.

Small changes count. Ask for help if sleep stays difficult.
