Page 4 · Support & sources

You do not have to manage it alone.

Reach out to someone you trust—a friend, family member, teacher, college counsellor or health professional. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Helplines in India

Numbers and availability can change. Please verify current details through an official government source before relying on them.

Tele-MANAS · Government of India

14416

Government mental-health support. Check an official source for current service hours, languages and availability.

KIRAN · verify current status

1800-599-0019

This number has been listed for the KIRAN mental-health helpline. Verify that it is active before relying on it.

Immediate emergency

112

For immediate danger, contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.

If calling feels difficult, you could begin: “I’m a student and I haven’t been okay. Can you help me work out a next step?”
